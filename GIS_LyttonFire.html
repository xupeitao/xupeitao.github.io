BC Wildfire Impact Assessment (2021 Lytton Fire)

Assessing vegetation loss and fire severity through satellite imagery and change analysis.

01 / 專案概覽

Understanding forest disturbance after wildfire

This project evaluated vegetation loss and fire severity associated with the 2021 Lytton Fire in British Columbia. Satellite imagery and NDVI/NBR analysis were used to quantify and map forest disturbance impacts.

The assessment compares Landsat 8 imagery from 2020 and 2021 around Lytton and Lillooet. NDVI comparison highlights changes in vegetation greenness, while NBR comparison supports the assessment of burn severity. The four map sheets present the imagery and analysis in sequence.
