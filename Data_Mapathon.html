01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Mapping contributions across communities

This Tableau dashboard presents the 2026 OPS GIS Summit Mapathon, held from April 20 to May 1, 2026. It connects Ontario contributors and cities with humanitarian mapping projects around the world.

Maps, contribution flows, contributor summaries and cumulative activity charts bring the collective effort into one visual story, showing where people contributed and how participation developed over time.
