Term 2 Forest Inventory Analysis and Tableau Dashboard Project

Connecting forest inventory data, spatial analysis and interactive dashboards to explore Ontario's forests.

01 / 專案概覽

From forest inventory to interactive insights

Ontario's Term 2 Forest Resources Inventory (FRI) provides spatial and tabular information for understanding forests at the Forest Management Unit (FMU) level. This project brings inventory data into interactive Tableau dashboards for forest-level exploration and comparisons across inventories.

Working with eight Crown Forest inventories, I validated and analyzed spatial and tabular data using ArcGIS Pro and Python, and developed dashboards to communicate the results. The workflow connects data ingestion, validation and transformation with cloud storage, data modelling and Tableau delivery.
