From wildfire records to an interactive story

I applied SQL for tabular and spatial data management, and ArcGIS StoryMaps for visualization, to explore Ontario forest fire data from the 2010s.

The project emphasizes data standards and interactive products to support evidence-based planning and communicate geographic information to non-technical audiences.
