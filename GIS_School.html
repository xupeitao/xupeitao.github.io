GIS / SCHOOL PLANNING & ACCESSIBILITY

School Zone and Enrolment Insights in Ontario

Exploring school enrolment, demographics and access to education through spatial analysis.

01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Understanding schools in their geographic context

This project explores Ontario school data alongside demographic and geographic information to understand enrolment patterns, school accessibility and potential locations for new schools.

Spatial, network and machine learning analyses support the study of population density, service areas and resource allocation. The ArcGIS dashboard brings the results together through interactive maps and charts, while the presentation below documents the project.
