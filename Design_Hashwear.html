    03 / A NOTE FROM ME

    It started with a little sprout.

    These little accessories began as a playful detail on my homepage portrait. Hashwear gives them a place of their own: an ongoing collection of illustrations to collect, combine and try on.

    The headwear series is called Hashhat. Here, those same little things can also sit on a shoulder or become a cheek decoration. Start with my portrait, or make a look with your own photo.

    Collection format inspired by Jane Manchun Wong’s Hashflags.
